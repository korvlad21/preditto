# Skill: Research Mode

## Purpose

Provides a compact project research mode without overloading the context or trying to cover everything at once.

## When to Use

- when the user asks to research the project or an unfamiliar area;
- when a new thematic investigation is needed rather than a targeted fix;
- when the backlog, coverage, or next research step needs to be updated.

## What It Covers

- restoring research context;
- selecting a small area to investigate;
- recording confirmed facts;
- updating the research state.

## Workflow

1. Restore context from:
   - `AI_LEARN/system_state.md`;
   - `AI_LEARN/current_iteration.md`;
   - `AI_LEARN/research/research_backlog.md`;
   - `AI_LEARN/research/coverage_map.md`.

2. Select one small area of the project that can realistically be verified during the current iteration.

3. Open only the artifacts required for that area.

4. Separate confirmed facts from working hypotheses.

5. Store knowledge only after linking it to real files, routes, classes, tables, or confirmed executions.

6. After the iteration, update `current_iteration.md`, `system_state.md`, and, when necessary, `AI_LEARN/research/research_backlog.md` or `AI_LEARN/research/coverage_map.md`.

7. Record the next small step.

For a large task that has already been approved, repeat this cycle in smaller parts until the agreed scope is completed. Saving intermediate state and reducing context do not mean that the user's task is complete.

## Do Not

- do not try to map the entire project in a single pass;
- do not replace research with a summary of unverified assumptions;
- do not create new thematic files without useful confirmed content;
- do not finish a research iteration without updating the state and recording the next step.
